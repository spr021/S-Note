## Summary (Chrome, max 132 characters)

Take notes, highlights, comments, sticky marks, and drawings on any website.
Everything stays on your device.

## Detailed description

S Note adds a private annotation layer on top of every website. Highlight a
passage, leave a comment, drop a sticky note, or draw with the pen — then hide
the layer and keep browsing normally. Your notes come back the next time you
visit the page.

Everything is stored locally in your browser. S Note has no account, no server,
and no analytics.

**Features**

- Plain notes linked to the current page
- A toggleable annotation layer that never modifies the website's own content
- A locked note-taking mode where links, buttons, forms, and shortcuts cannot run
- Select-text, highlight, comment, sticky-mark, pen, and erase tools
- Four highlight colors: yellow, green, blue, and pink
- Google Docs-style comments anchored to selected text
- Draggable sticky notes positioned anywhere on a page
- Scroll-aware freehand pen strokes
- Undo plus keyboard shortcuts for every tool
- Automatic restoration of annotations when you revisit a page
- A quote-and-context fallback when a site's layout changes
- "This page" and "All notes" views, grouped by website
- Light and dark popup themes
- Right-click actions for highlighting and commenting

**How it works**

1. Open any normal `http` or `https` page.
2. Click the floating S button, or choose "Unhide notes" in the popup.
3. While notes are visible the page is locked, so you can annotate without
   triggering the site. Scrolling, selecting text, and the right-click menu
   stay available.
4. Hide the layer to return the page to normal.

**Privacy**

S Note does not collect, transmit, or sell any data. Your notes live in your
browser's local storage for your profile only. See the privacy policy for
details.

**Support**

S Note is free. If it helps you, you can buy the developer a coffee from the
extension's Settings tab. Tips are optional.
