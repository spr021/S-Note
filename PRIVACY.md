# S Note — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

S Note is a local-first browser extension. It does **not** collect, transmit,
sell, or share your data with anyone.

## What S Note stores

Your notes, highlights, comments, sticky marks, and drawings are saved in your
browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`) for the current
browser profile. They never leave your device.

## What S Note does not do

- It does not send your notes or browsing content to any server.
- It does not use analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or advertising.
- It does not sell or share data with third parties.
- It does not read pages other than the page you are actively annotating.

## Permissions

- **storage** — save your notes locally in the browser.
- **activeTab** — act on the page you are viewing when you use S Note.
- **scripting** — inject the S Note annotation layer into pages you annotate.
- **contextMenus** — add right-click actions for highlighting and commenting.

## Donations

S Note is free. The optional "Buy me a coffee" link opens
`https://buymeacoffee.com/spr021` in a new tab. Any payment is handled entirely
by Buy Me a Coffee on their own website; S Note does not process payments and
does not receive any payment or personal data. Donations are voluntary and are
received by the developer, not by Google or Mozilla.

## Data removal

Removing the extension removes its local data, depending on your browser's
extension-data handling. You can also delete individual notes, or all notes for
a website, from the S Note popup.
